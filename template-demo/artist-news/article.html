Nova Rey announces the Midnight Frequency Tour

Fourteen cities, one continuous stage design and a ticket window that opens a full day early for newsletter subscribers.

By Team Nova ·Sep 5, 2026 ·4 min read ·Updated Sep 6, 2026

Share

In this story

  • Presale rules

Tour dates

Nova Rey will tour the Midnight Frequency show through fourteen cities between October 2026 and February 2027, with the first six dates confirmed today and the remainder announced in batches as venues clear their calendars.

The run opens at Lotus Arena in Ho Chi Minh City on 12 October and closes the first leg at The Wiltern in Los Angeles on 16 November. Tickets for the confirmed dates go on general sale at 10:00 local time, with one important exception described below.

The routing

The first leg stays deliberately tight: five Asian cities in five weeks, then a single North American date to close. That is a routing decision rather than a demand one — the stage design travels as one piece, and the crew wanted the shortest possible distance between load-outs while the show settles.

“We would rather play the same room twice than move the rig twice,” the production manager wrote in the tour note sent to subscribers this morning. It is why Los Angeles picked up a second night before any other city was added.

The rig is not decoration. It is a second narrator, and it was written before a single note was mixed.

Presale rules

Newsletter subscribers receive the ticket link and access code 24 hours before general on-sale. There is no paid fan club tier, no partner resale platform and no dynamic pricing on any date of this tour.

  • Presale codes are emailed and are single-use.
  • Sold-out shows open a waiting list on this site rather than a resale page.
  • Accessible seating is booked through each venue directly — the venue links are on the tour page.

The stage

The Midnight Frequency rig was built at the same time as the record. Its cue list follows the album’s running order closely enough that the lighting designer and the mixing engineer worked from the same session file for six weeks — a detail covered in the studio diary published last week.
