The atelier

Formulated in SoHo, tested on nobody

Our lab sits above the Greene Street store. Every formula is developed in-house by a team of four chemists and a trichologist, then trialled on 200 volunteers across three hair types before it earns a bottle.

We publish the full INCI list and the active percentage for every product — because "clinically proven" means nothing without a number next to it.
