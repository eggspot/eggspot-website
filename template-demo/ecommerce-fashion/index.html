Our story

Built in Portland, worn everywhere

We started in 2016 with one frustration: good basics had quietly disappeared. Everything was either disposable or absurdly priced, and almost nothing was made to survive a second season.

So we built the closet we wanted. Twelve partner factories, mills we can name, and a small line of pieces we refine year after year instead of replacing every quarter.
