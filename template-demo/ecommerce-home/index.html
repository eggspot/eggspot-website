The workshop

Built two hours from where the trees fell

Every board we use is milled within 150 miles of Asheville, kiln-dried in our own kilns for six weeks, then cut, joined and finished by a team of eleven makers.

No veneer, no particleboard, no mystery hardware. If a joint ever fails, send it back and we will fix it — that offer has no expiry date.
