✓ Free shipping over $75 · 30-day returns · Ships in 2–3 weeks

Each top is cut from a single black walnut slab sourced within 150 miles of the workshop, so no two tables share a grain pattern. Legs are tapered by hand and joined with draw-bored mortise and tenon — a joint that tightens as the wood moves rather than loosening.

Dimensions: 48"W × 24"D × 17"H. Weight 62 lb. Arrives fully assembled.
