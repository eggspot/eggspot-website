Dropped on purpose.

CASELAB started in a Brooklyn studio with one rule: nothing ships until it survives concrete. The Christmas range is held to exactly the same standard.

The story

It started with a shattered screen.

In 2022 our founder dropped a week-old iPhone on a Brooklyn sidewalk and paid $329 to fix it. The case that failed was a $12 no-name — slippery, yellowed, and useless at the one job it had.

So we built a lab before we built a brand. Every CASELAB design is drawn in-house, printed on demand, and tortured on a drop rig until it passes 10 feet onto concrete. Clear cases sit in a UV chamber for the equivalent of two years of sunlight before we approve the material.

Today we ship from a US warehouse, wrap every gift bundle in recycled kraft and gold ribbon before it leaves, and still answer every support email like it's from a friend. In December that matters more than usual: the last dispatch that reaches a tree by Christmas leaves on Dec 18.

What we stand behind

Three promises, plus one for December.

Promise 01

Design in-house, always.

No stock art, no reselling someone else's prints. Every drop is drawn by our studio team and retired when it sells out.

Promise 02

Lifetime anti-yellow warranty.

If a clear CASELAB case ever yellows, we replace it free. No receipts, no interrogation.

Promise 03

Tested before hyped.

MIL-STD-810G drop rigs, UV chambers, MagSafe pull gauges. We publish the numbers on every product page.

Bonus

Wrapped before it ships.

Recycled kraft, a gold ribbon, a blank card, and no price anywhere on the parcel. Free on every gift bundle.
