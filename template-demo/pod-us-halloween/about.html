The story

It started with a shattered screen.

In 2022 our founder dropped a week-old iPhone on a Brooklyn sidewalk and paid $329 to fix it. The case that failed was a $12 no-name — slippery, yellowed, and useless at the one job it had.

So we built a lab before we built a brand. Every CASELAB design is drawn in-house, printed on demand, and tortured on a drop rig until it passes 10 feet onto concrete. Glow cases sit in a decay chamber until we can prove eight hours of visible green off a five-minute charge.

Today we ship from a US warehouse, retire each Halloween run on November 1, and still answer every support email like it's from a friend.
