Every tag is cut, deburred and engraved on East 2nd Street in Austin. We proof each name by eye before it ships, because a tag with a typo is a tag that does not work.

Brass, stainless steel, rose gold and blackened steel — all rolled in the United States, all thick enough to survive a decade of jumping into creeks.
