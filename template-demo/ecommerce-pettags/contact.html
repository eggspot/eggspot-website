Quick answers

Frequently asked

Orders placed before 1pm CT are engraved the same business day and ship the next. Most U.S. addresses receive their tag within two to four days of ordering. Need it faster? Call the workshop — we can usually cut in the queue.

Small (1.0in) suits cats and dogs under 15 lbs and holds about eight comfortable characters per line. Large (1.3in) suits anything over 15 lbs and stays readable with a full twelve. If your pet is on the line, size up — the text engraves bigger.

Personalized items are final sale — we cannot resell a tag with your dog's name on it. Non-personalized items (collars, gift boxes, blank tags) come with 30-day returns. If we engraved something differently from what you typed, we recut and reship it free, no return needed.
