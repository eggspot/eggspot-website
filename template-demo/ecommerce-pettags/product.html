✓ Free shipping over $35 · Engraved and shipped in 2 business days

Our most-ordered tag: 1.0in (Small) or 1.3in (Large) of solid brass, tumbled smooth and engraved on both faces. Ships with a stainless split ring; cat orders ship with a breakaway ring instead. Name on the front, phone number on the back — or tell us to flip it in the order notes.

  • Up to 12 characters on line 1 and 16 on line 2 — shorter text engraves larger and reads from further away.
  • Skip your pet's address. A name and one reachable phone number is what gets pets home fastest.
  • Script looks beautiful on Small tags but reads slower — pick Classic or Modern for a nervous escape artist.
  • We engrave exactly what you type, capitalization included. Personalized tags are final sale.

  • Free standard shipping on U.S. orders over $35 (2–4 business days), $4.95 below that.
  • Orders placed before 1pm CT are engraved the same business day.
  • 30-day returns on non-personalized items. Engraved tags are final sale — but if we misspell it, we recut it free.
